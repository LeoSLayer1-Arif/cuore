Contactless vital signs

Measure your heart rate with just your webcam.

Cuore reads the tiny colour changes that blood flow causes in your face. Sit still for about a minute and get your average heart rate, with breathing and blink rate alongside.

Camera opens only after you click.

  1. 1

    Get ready

    Face a window or lamp. Avoid strong light behind you.

  2. 2

    Calibrate, 10 s

    Hold still while the signal settles.

  3. 3

    Measure, 1 min

    Your average heart rate is recorded and shown at the end.

Cuore is a screening demo, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
