Contactless vitals

Your body keeps
a quiet rhythm.
CUORE listens.

A webcam is enough. CUORE reads your heart rate, breathing and blinking, learns what's normal for you in about a minute, then shows you when you drift from it.

≈ 70 s · camera required

  1. 01Skin colourEach heartbeat changes your skin's colour very slightly. The camera can see it; you can't.
  2. 02Chest motionBreathing moves your shoulders. CUORE tracks that motion frame to frame.
  3. 03Eye landmarks478 points on your face. When your eyelids close and open, that's a blink.
  • Video is processed on this computer and never uploaded.
  • Your browser asks before the camera turns on.
  • For awareness, not diagnosis. CUORE is not a medical device.
