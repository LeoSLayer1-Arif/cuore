02How it works

One camera frame, three independent signals.

Every frame takes the same path. Each signal has its own pipeline and its own quality check, so one weak signal never holds the others back.

  1. A

    Camera frame

    Your browser captures video and sends each frame, stamped with its capture time, to the CUORE server on this computer.

    1280 × 720 ideal · 30 fps ideal

  2. B

    Face landmarks

    A face-landmark model places 478 points on your face. They locate the skin, the eyes and the area below your chin.

    MediaPipe Face Landmarker · 1 face

  3. C

    Three measurements

    Skin colourPulse wave → heart rate
    Chest motionOptical flow → breathing
    Eye opennessLandmarks → blinks
  4. D

    Quality checks

    Each value carries its own verdict: reliable, provisional or unavailable. Nothing is shown as more certain than it is.

    Heart quality > 0.15 · breathing SNR ≥ −3 dB

  5. E

    Baseline, then composure

    Reliable readings build your personal baseline. Once two or more are ready, CUORE compares the present with it.

    60 s per baseline · 30 s of valid readings

04Personal baselines

Normal is personal. CUORE starts from yours.

Resting heart rate, breathing pace and blink rate differ a lot from one person to the next. A fixed “healthy range” says little about whether you have changed. So CUORE compares you with you, measured in the same chair, light and session.

60 s
Minimum time since a measurement's first valid reading.
30 s
Minimum valid readings inside that time. Elapsed time alone never counts.
×3
Independent baselines: a weak heart signal never blocks breathing or blinks.

What CUORE can't do

  • It is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything.
  • It can't see much in dim light, or with a bright window behind you.
  • Talking and moving disturb breathing and heart readings.
  • Composure is a comparison with your own baseline, not a measure of stress.

06The science

Three established techniques, used carefully.

Remote photoplethysmography · rPPG

Blood volume changes skin colour.

With each heartbeat, more blood reaches the skin and absorbs slightly more light, especially green light. Across many video frames that faint change forms a pulse wave. Researchers showed in 2008 that ordinary ambient light and a consumer camera are enough. CUORE uses a neural rPPG model, FacePhys, through the open-source open-rppg library, and reports its signal quality alongside every reading.

Verkruysse, Svaasand & Nelson, “Remote plethysmographic imaging using ambient light”, Optics Express, 2008.

Optical flow

Motion between frames reveals breathing.

Optical flow estimates how each small patch of an image moves from one frame to the next. CUORE measures the vertical motion of the area below your chin with Farnebäck's dense method, then finds the dominant slow rhythm in the last 30 seconds. If that rhythm isn't clearly above the noise, no number is shown.

Farnebäck, “Two-frame motion estimation based on polynomial expansion”, SCIA, 2003.

Facial landmarks

Eyelid geometry counts blinks.

A face-landmark model places 478 points on the face in every frame. The distance between eyelid points relative to eye width gives an openness ratio, which drops sharply during a blink. CUORE measures your own open level and counts a blink when openness falls below 75 % of it for a few frames.

Soukupová & Čech, “Real-Time Eye Blink Detection using Facial Landmarks”, CVWW, 2016.

07Privacy

Your face stays on your computer.

CUORE has no cloud service and no account. The measuring app and its server both run on your computer, and every claim below can be checked in the source.

  • Local server onlyThe CUORE server listens on 127.0.0.1, so other devices on your network can't reach it.
  • Frames are processed, not keptVideo frames are analysed in memory. The server writes no images or video to disk.
  • The browser asks firstThe camera only turns on after you allow it, and it stops when you end the session.
  • No third parties on the pageNo analytics, trackers, external fonts or CDNs. A strict content security policy blocks loading anything from elsewhere.
  • Exports are numbersThe optional diagnostics export is a CSV of readings and quality values, never images.
  • This websiteheartware.health is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics or trackers and never receives camera video; like any website, the host keeps standard access logs (such as IP address and time).

09Questions

Before you sit down.

01Is CUORE a medical device?

No. It is an experimental tool for personal awareness. Its readings are estimates from a webcam and shouldn't be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

02Why does it need about two minutes?

Each measurement needs its own window (10 s for heart rate, 20–30 s for breathing, 60 s for blinks), and each baseline needs 60 s after its first valid reading with at least 30 s of valid data. Shortening those windows would make the numbers less trustworthy.

03What is the 45-second quick read?

An experimental short session. Heart rate and usually breathing can be measured in 45 s; blink rate stays provisional, and no baseline or composure result is possible. The report says so plainly.

04My heart signal says “weak”. What helps?

Face a steady light source, avoid a bright window behind you, sit still and keep your face in frame. Breathing and blinks keep measuring independently in the meantime.

05Does my video go to a server?

Only to the CUORE server running on your own computer at 127.0.0.1. It analyses frames in memory and doesn't save them. There is no remote server.

06Does it work on my phone?

Yes, through home Wi-Fi mode. Start CUORE on your computer with --lan, open the address it prints on your phone, and enter the pairing code. Your phone's video goes to your own computer over your Wi-Fi, is processed in memory there, and is not sent anywhere else. Measuring entirely on the phone, without a computer, is planned.

07What does “composure” mean?

How your current readings compare with your own baseline. “Elevated” means your blink rate fell well below your baseline, or two signals shifted together. It is deliberately not called stress.
