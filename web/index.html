CUORE estimates your heart rate, breathing and blinking from your camera, learns your own baseline over about a minute, then tells you when you drift from it.

  • Video is processed on this computer and never leaves it.
  • Your browser will ask for camera permission.
  • CUORE is not a medical device. Don't use it for diagnosis.
