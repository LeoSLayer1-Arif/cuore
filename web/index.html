Your webcam reads heart rate, breathing and blinks. Readings appear as soon as each one is ready, and CUORE learns your personal baseline while you sit.

Pair this device

CUORE is running in home Wi-Fi mode. Enter the pairing code shown in the terminal on the computer running CUORE.

Session length

Why the difference: each baseline needs 60 s after its first valid reading, and the blink count needs a full 60 s window.
